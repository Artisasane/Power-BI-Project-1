### **Objective**  
**Title:** Understanding the Sales Performance  
✅ Track total sales, revenue, and profitability  
✅ Identify top-selling products and companies  
✅ Analyze customer behavior and purchasing trends  
✅ Discover high-performing regions and channels  
✅ Optimize marketing and sales strategies  

- The primary objective of this dashboard is to help businesses **make data-driven decisions** by analyzing **key sales and customer insights**.

### **Product Profitability**

### **Top 10 Sales Regions**

### **Customer Activity Analysis**  
**Title:** When Do Customers Buy the Most?  
📊 **Customer Activity by Weekday (Pie Chart)**  

**Insights:**  
- **Friday (15.44%) and Saturday (18.12%)** are peak shopping days.  
- **Tuesday (10.74%) has the lowest activity.**  

- Running promotions on **Fridays and Saturdays** could boost sales further.
